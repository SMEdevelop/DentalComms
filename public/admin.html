Office name

Shown at the top of everyone's screen.

Stations

One for each computer or room — e.g. “Front Desk”, “Room 1”, “Lab”.

Quick-message buttons

The big buttons staff tap to send a message. Pick a color for each. “Urgent” makes the chime louder and more frequent. Use ▲ ▼ to change the order they appear in — the top one here is the first button on everyone’s screen.

How alerts announce themselves

What happens on the screen that receives a message. This is the same for every station.

Flashing is worth leaving on where a tablet may be muted, or the room is noisy. Turn both off and a message still appears on screen and waits — it just does so quietly.
